Option 1: Build & GrowFor smaller brands

The 3 week pilot test.

Before you invest in Europe, we test the demand for your product with real European buyers. CBX Germany charges no fee for the pilot. You see real numbers and decide afterwards if and how you want to continue.

  1. Week 1

    Build and launch

    Landing page in German, 2 to 3 ad creatives and pixel tracking. Meta campaigns go live.

  2. Week 2

    Optimize

    We adjust audiences, messages and creatives based on the first data.

  3. Week 3

    Report

    CTR, cost per click, visits and purchase intent through a waitlist, with a clear recommendation.

Ad budget. Paid by you directly through your own ad account. The amount is agreed with you personally.

No products are sold during the pilot. Buyers only join a waitlist and no real orders are placed. That is why stock, shipping and EU product compliance do not apply during the pilot yet.

After the pilot

  1. 1

    Results call

    We go through the numbers together and answer one question: is there real demand for your products in Europe?

  2. 2

    Next steps

    If the answer is yes, we propose the next steps that fit your brand, for example a store, creator content, ads, TikTok Shop or a warehouse in Germany. Scope and price are agreed individually.

  3. 3

    Next level

    Once your brand has grown, Option 2 opens up: exclusive access to Temu and TikTok Shop.

If the numbers are weak, you have lost nothing but a small ad budget, and you know more about Europe than before.

Your brand is strong in China and ready for Europe. Most brands sign up to Temu and TikTok Shop on their own and start as one seller among thousands. With CBX Germany your brand skips that line: we connect you directly with our exclusive partners at Temu and TikTok and set up everything you need to sell in Europe from day one.

What you get

  • Exclusive Temu and TikTok Shop partners

    A direct connection to the partners we work with, not the standard sign up.

EU complianceBoth options

Compliant from day one in Europe.

A product that cannot be sold legally in the EU cannot be sold at all. We introduce your brand to specialised European partners and add no markup on their fees.

  • Packaging and environment

    Packaging and EPR registration, including electronics and batteries.

  • Technical product compliance

    Technical file, GPSR, CE, food contact and textile labelling.

  • Cosmetics and personal care

    Safety report, CPNP notification, EU responsible person and label check.

Clear responsibility. Your brand stays the seller and importer and keeps the legal responsibility. You contract and pay the partners directly. CBX Germany introduces suitable partners and does not take on operational responsibility.

FAQ

What brands ask first.

Which option fits my brand?

Smaller brands start with Option 1. Larger brands that are ready for Europe go with Option 2. We decide together in the first call.

Is the pilot test really free?

Yes, our work during the 3 weeks is free of charge. You only pay the ad budget directly to Meta through your own ad account.

What happens after the pilot?

We go through the results with you. If there is demand, we propose next steps and agree scope and price individually. There is no obligation.

Can you get us onto Temu or TikTok Shop?

We connect fitting brands personally with our contacts and support the process. The final decision is made by the platform.

Who handles EU compliance?

You carry the legal responsibility. We introduce you to specialised partners and add no markup.

Are you a distributor?

No. We are a service provider. We do not buy or resell products. You stay the seller and importer.

Who owns the store and ad accounts?

You do. We only get the access we need for the agreed work.

Can a brand move from Option 1 to Option 2?

Yes. Once your brand has grown and the numbers are right, we connect you with our Temu and TikTok Shop partners.
